# 🛍️ ShopPilot AI

> AI-powered e-commerce platform for smarter shopping and business growth.

ShopPilot AI is a modern full-stack e-commerce platform that combines traditional online shopping with AI-powered product discovery, personalized recommendations, real-time commerce, and intelligent business insights.

The goal is to solve two sides of e-commerce:

- 🛒 Help customers find the right products faster.
- 📊 Help merchants understand their business and make smarter decisions.

## 🎯 Problem

Many e-commerce platforms provide basic shopping functionality but do not make product discovery and business decision-making intelligent.

Customers can struggle to find relevant products, while merchants often have large amounts of data but limited actionable insights.

## 💡 Solution

ShopPilot AI combines e-commerce with artificial intelligence.

Customers can describe what they want naturally, while merchants can use AI to understand products, reviews, sales, and inventory.
